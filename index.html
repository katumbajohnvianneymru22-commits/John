Healthy rabbits, better future

How We Care for Rabbits

Consistent daily care helps rabbits stay healthy and comfortable. Their needs can vary with age, breed and condition.

Our care promise

Care is part of every day.

We believe rabbits deserve clean surroundings, suitable food, safe housing and gentle handling. Observing them regularly helps us notice when something may be wrong.

Clean Water

Make fresh, clean drinking water available at all times. Check and clean water containers daily.

Suitable Feed

Provide suitable hay or grass and balanced rabbit feed. Introduce new foods gradually.

Clean Housing

Keep the rabbit house dry, clean and well ventilated. Remove waste regularly.

Enough Space

Provide secure housing with room to move, stretch and rest, protected from weather and predators.

Health Observation

Check appetite, behaviour, movement and droppings. Seek veterinary help if a rabbit appears unwell.

Gentle Handling

Approach calmly and support the rabbit's body properly. Never lift a rabbit by its ears.

About John Vianney Katumba

My name is John Vianney Katumba. Since my childhood, I have had a strong love for rabbits. That passion inspired me to establish Vianney Rabbit Farm.

To me, rabbit rearing is more than just a business. I also love keeping rabbits as pets, and I value the care, patience and responsibility involved in raising them.

My goal is to turn my love for rabbits into a meaningful farming venture while continuing to care for and learn about these wonderful animals.
